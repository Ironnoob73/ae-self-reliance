# Applied Energistics: Crystal Industries (AECI)

**Applied Energistics: Crystal Industries (AECI)** is an expansion mod for *Applied Energistics: Crystal Science (AECS)* that adds a variety of blocks and items—inspired by other industrial mods and factory-style games—to *Applied Energistics 2*. Its goal is to fulfill most industrial requirements, provided that *Applied Energistics 2* and its associated add-on mods are installed.
